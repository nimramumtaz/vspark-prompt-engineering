Challenge format

Brief in. Prompts out. Judged.

Three moves, one clock. Everything you submit is created during the competition — nothing pre-made.

01

Receive the brief

At the starting whistle you get the scenario, reference material and the exact deliverable list for your category.

02

Engineer and iterate

Write structured prompts, generate, inspect and refine. Keep every prompt — the log is part of your score.

03

Submit and defend

Upload the final output and your prompt log before time is called, then walk the judges through your process.

Rules & judging

Fair play, then fine craft

Prompt Engineering rewards control, not luck. Judges review the final output and the prompts behind it, and their evaluation is final.

  • Individual entry or a team of maximum two members
  • Exactly the category time limit, including generation, editing and submission
  • Students bring and use their own laptops
  • Free, Pro and Premium AI platforms allowed — participants arrange their own accounts
  • Use clear, structured prompts and refine them for consistency and quality
  • No copied work, no fabricated generation evidence, no harmful or unauthorised content
Judges may inspect your prompts and verify how the output was produced. Previously completed work cannot be submitted.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Who can register for Prompt Engineering?
Any student currently enrolled in a college or university in Pakistan. You can enter individually or as a team of up to two members. One registration covers one category entry.
Do I have to choose a category now?
Yes — pick video generation, website generation, image generation or image editing in the registration form. The category determines your brief, your time limit and the judging panel. Contact the organisers if you need to change it before registration closes.
Which AI tools are allowed?
Free, Pro and Premium tools are all allowed — for example Runway, Kling, Pika, Sora, CapCut and Flow for video; Midjourney, Firefly or similar for images; and AI builders or code assistants for websites. AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude can help you write prompts. You arrange your own accounts and credits.
What exactly do I submit?
Your final output in the format stated in the brief — a playable MP4, a live website preview, or an image set with before/after files — plus the prompt log that produced it. Everything must be created during the competition window.
How is the PKR 1,000 fee paid?
Submit the form to reserve your entry, then pay at the registration desk on event day. The fee covers the whole team, not each member. The organisers verify every entry from the admin portal before confirming your seat.
How do I know my registration reached the organisers?
Every submission receives a unique team code (for example PE-26-0007). Use the Track Status page with your code and leader email to see live approval and payment status, and any note from the organisers.
